Connecting…

Choose the headphones, speakers or virtual output your music plays through. A shared output includes every app playing through it. Your choice is remembered. It reconnects automatically next time you open the app. Stop audio keeps it stopped until you start it again.

Audio stays in memory on this computer. No microphone access, recordings or uploads. Song details remain available when audio is stopped. The selected output is saved only on this computer. Windows startup is optional in the tray menu and starts disabled.

The bridge stays in your system tray when you close this page. Use its Quit menu to stop it. Metadata and audio share port 5000.
